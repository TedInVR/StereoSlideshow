VERSION 0.15 — EXPANDED ORGANIZER, MUSIC PLAYLIST AND TIMING

+ Expand lists opens a maximized organizer with photo/video and music lists.
Both support drag reordering and explicit move buttons. Return/Save commits;
Cancel leaves the project unchanged. The main music list is also reorderable.

Multiple ordered music tracks, with per-track and total m:ss and seconds.
Duration reads run in a background worker with caching.

Two ending modes: visual sequence or music playlist. Visual-ending repeats
the entire ordered playlist if needed. Music-ending plays one playlist pass,
with the closing fade inside its duration and no additional black tail.
Manual music-ending can trim later visuals or hold the final displayed frame;
the calculator reports this explicitly.

Automatic fitting reserves title/opening, full video clips, closing fade and
divides the remainder equally among photos, with one-frame rounding adjustment.
Impossible fits stop before encoding. Exports normalize/recheck the decoded
playlist length before building a music-ending timeline.

Calculator and playlist supported in the Guided Walkthrough. Existing single
music settings migrate to a one-track playlist. Permanent app identity and
settings location are unchanged.

TESTED
Frame-based calculations and impossible budgets; ordered two-tone soundtrack;
whole-playlist repetition; music-ending hold and trim; auto-fit duration;
SBS/anaglyph decode; odd-width photo regression; mixed photos/full video with
retained audio; Qt organizer expansion, order changes, Cancel isolation,
endpoint controls, duration display and walkthrough playlist preservation.

VERSION 0.14 — ODD-WIDTH PHOTOS AND READABLE ERRORS

Odd-width SBS photos are accepted. Rendering uses equal eye crops, omitting
the single center seam column. The original file is never written or changed.
Even-width input crops are unchanged.

Every photo is fully decoded and checked before the video encoder starts.
Video metadata and the music track are also checked before rendering.
Corrupt or missing inputs now fail early with a short filename-specific message.

Raw processing logs no longer appear as popup text or in the status label.
Errors use a bounded dialog with a visible Close button, Escape dismissal,
and optional scrollable technical details. Full diagnostics are saved under
Logs/render_error_TIMESTAMP.txt. An Open Logs folder button makes sharing
an error report easier. Cancellation updates status without another modal.

Tested exact odd/even eye cropping, odd-width JPEG render with WAV music,
SBS/anaglyph decoding, unchanged source bytes, corrupt-photo detection before
encoding, concise FFmpeg failures, and oversized-error dialog behavior with
large diagnostics. Qt offscreen tests verified Close visibility and Escape.
